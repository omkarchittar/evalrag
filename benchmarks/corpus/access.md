# Meridian Labs — Access

Fictional benchmark policy. Version 2026-09. These are authored test documents, not real company policies.

## Rule 1

Contractor accounts expire after 90 days unless renewed by their sponsor.

## Rule 2

Access reviews take place quarterly.

## Rule 3

Administrator accounts must use hardware security keys.

## Rule 4

Interactive login sessions expire after 12 hours.

## Rule 5

Production access requires an access ticket and approval from the service owner.

## Rule 6

Departing staff accounts must be disabled within 4 hours of the recorded departure time.
