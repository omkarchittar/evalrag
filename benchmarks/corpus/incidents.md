# Meridian Labs — Incidents

Fictional benchmark policy. Version 2026-09. These are authored test documents, not real company policies.

## Rule 1

SEV1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.

## Rule 2

During a SEV1 incident, public status updates are issued every 30 minutes.

## Rule 3

SEV2 incidents must be acknowledged within 60 minutes.

## Rule 4

The on-call engineer initially acts as incident commander.

## Rule 5

A postmortem is due within 3 business days after incident resolution.

## Rule 6

Incident reviews are blameless and focus on system improvements.
