# Meridian Labs — Vendors

Fictional benchmark policy. Version 2026-09. These are authored test documents, not real company policies.

## Rule 1

Vendor onboarding requires a tax form, an insurance certificate, and a bank confirmation.

## Rule 2

An insurance certificate must remain valid for at least 30 days at approval time.

## Rule 3

Requesters cannot approve their own vendor requests.

## Rule 4

Duplicate vendors are detected by normalized tax identifier.

## Rule 5

High-risk vendors undergo a review every 12 months.

## Rule 6

Vendor creation is permitted only after an independent approver accepts the current request revision.
