# Meridian Labs — Delivery

Fictional benchmark policy. Version 2026-09. These are authored test documents, not real company policies.

## Rule 1

A production rollout progresses through 5 percent, 25 percent, and 100 percent of traffic.

## Rule 2

Each rollout stage is monitored for 30 minutes before advancing.

## Rule 3

Rollback is required if p95 latency increases by more than 20 percent relative to baseline.

## Rule 4

Rollback is required if the error rate increases by more than 2 percentage points.

## Rule 5

Database migrations must remain backward compatible with the previous application version.

## Rule 6

Routine releases occur on weekdays between 09:00 and 16:00 UTC.
